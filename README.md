# 🚀 TechPath AI

### AI-Powered Career Guidance & Industry Readiness Platform

TechPath AI is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed to help engineering students understand their career options, identify suitable technology paths, and prepare themselves for the software industry.

What started as a simple idea for an FAQ chatbot evolved into a more complete AI-powered career companion that can interact with students, answer their questions, provide career guidance, and help them explore relevant technologies and learning paths.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 AI Career Assistant
An interactive AI chatbot designed to answer students' career, technology, and industry-related questions.

### 💬 Aira & Arin AI Chat
The central conversational feature of TechPath AI.

Aira and Arin AI Chat provide an interactive experience where students can ask questions and receive AI-powered responses related to:

- Career guidance
- Programming technologies
- Software development
- AI and emerging technologies
- Learning paths
- Industry preparation

### 🧭 Tech Path Guidance
Helps students explore different technology domains and understand what skills and technologies they can learn for their desired career path.

### 📚 Learning Guidance
Provides structured recommendations for technologies and skills based on the student's interests and career goals.

### 🎯 Industry Readiness
Designed to help students understand the skills required to become more prepared for real-world software development opportunities.

### 📱 Responsive Interface
Designed to provide a clean and interactive experience across different screen sizes.
